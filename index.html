How it works

Three steps. One text starts it.

Built for the specific ask: fuel and ice by 1pm, a diver next week, a part picked up before the weekend. Text it and we take it from there.

  1. Text us what you need

    Boat name, marina, slip, what needs doing and by when. A text is fastest; the short form below reaches the same people.

  2. We confirm what we can do, and when

    One reply: what we’ll handle, a ready-by time, and the fee. If it’s outside what we do, we say so and point you to someone who does it well.

  3. It’s handled

    You get a text when it’s done, with a note of what was done and who came aboard, if anyone did.

Questions

Straight answers.

If yours isn’t here, text it to us. You’ll get a plain reply.

How far ahead should we text?

As early as you know. Scheduled orders can be set up in advance, and same-day requests are welcome. Either way, our reply tells you what we can do and by when before anything starts, so you can plan around it.

What does it cost?

Each request is quoted on its own before we start, typically $75–150 depending on scope. Anything we buy for you, from provisions to fuel, is added at cost with the receipts. No packages, no hourly clock.

What if you notice something wrong aboard?

We flag it to the captain at once, and we stop there. We don’t repair and we don’t have a repair team. If you want it handled, we line up a trusted local vendor and coordinate the visit.

Are you insured? Who comes aboard?

We’re insured for boat access, and we’ll send the certificate whenever you ask. Every request is logged: what was asked, what was done, when, and who came aboard if anyone did. It’s a small local team we’ve vetted ourselves.

Which marinas do you cover?

We’re launching at South Beach Harbor, Pier 39 Marina and the San Francisco Marina. A marina shows as serving on the chart only once we’ve confirmed dock access there. Somewhere else? Text us and we’ll tell you where things stand.

What won’t you do?

We don’t repair, and we don’t sell trips or charters. If a request is outside what we do, we say so and point you to someone who does it well.

How do we pay, and do we get receipts?

You agree the quote by text before we start. When it’s done you get a note of what was done, with receipts for every purchase, which are passed on at cost.
